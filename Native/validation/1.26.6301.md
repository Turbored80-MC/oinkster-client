## Static survey

`Tools/SurveySignatures.py` was run against the executable with both Oinkster's
patterns and the checked-in Flarial signature table. All three Oinkster screen
and world-entry patterns from 1.26.5203 were absent. Nearly all referenced
Flarial game signatures were also absent; the only unique matches in that
survey were `ClientInstance::isPreGame`, `Json::Reader::Parse`,
`bgfx::rendercontextd3d12::submit`, and `mce::Color::BLACK`.

This is evidence of a broad compiler/layout change, not evidence that an old
address can be reused. Every game-memory adapter remains protected by its
unique-pattern and structural validation. A missing or ambiguous signature
must report the capability unavailable and must not install a hook.

## Release gate

- The package identity, executable digest, and protocol are confirmed.
- The generic DX12 overlay/injection path must be smoke-tested on this exact
  digest before publishing.
- Game-memory capabilities must be enabled only when their existing runtime
  validator succeeds on this build. Failed validators are expected to remain
  unavailable until a focused 26.63 adapter is derived.
- Packet capture/send and replay require explicit NetherNet-era live tests;
  do not infer compatibility from protocol parsing unit tests.
